## 📖 About The Business

**Solusi Rumah** is a leading national distributor of construction materials and furniture in Indonesia. We provide comprehensive solutions for residential and commercial construction projects with guaranteed quality products, integrated logistics, and expert consultation services.

### 🎯 Our Vision
To become Indonesia's most trusted partner in construction material distribution, delivering excellence in product quality, service, and nationwide accessibility.

### 🏢 Business Divisions

We operate across four major product categories:

#### 🪟 **Kaca & Aluminium** (Glass & Aluminum)
Modern facade solutions, doors, and windows for residential and commercial projects.
- Architectural glass systems
- Aluminum framing solutions
- Custom facade installations
- Storefront systems

#### 🏗️ **Baja Ringan & Atap** (Steel Frame & Roofing)
SNI-certified steel structures, galvalume, and roofing materials for long-term strength.
- Light steel framing systems
- Metal roofing materials
- Truss systems
- Insulation solutions

#### 🎨 **Keramik & Sanitasi** (Ceramics & Sanitation)
Premium collections of ceramics, granite, and modern bathroom fixtures.
- Floor and wall tiles
- Porcelain & granite
- Bathroom fixtures
- Sanitary ware

#### 🛋️ **Furniture & Perlengkapan Rumah** (Furniture & Home Essentials)
Latest design furniture and home essentials for your dream living space.
- Custom furniture
- Home decoration
- Kitchen cabinets
- Interior accessories

### 🌟 Our Competitive Advantages

- ✅ **Quality Guarantee** - Only SNI and ISO certified products
- 🚚 **Integrated Logistics** - Safe and timely delivery across Indonesia
- 💬 **Expert Consultation** - Professional team ready to calculate your project needs
- 🏆 **Proven Track Record** - Trusted by major contractors, developers, and hotel groups
- 🌏 **National Coverage** - Serving all provinces in Indonesia

## 🖥️ About This Website

This is a modern, responsive website showcasing Solusi Rumah's product portfolio, services, and project gallery. Built with cutting-edge web technologies to provide an excellent user experience.

### ✨ Features

- 📱 **Fully Responsive Design** - Optimized for all devices (mobile, tablet, desktop)
- 🎨 **Modern UI/UX** - Clean, professional design with smooth animations
- ⚡ **Fast Performance** - Optimized assets and efficient code structure
- 🔄 **Dynamic Components** - Reusable header and footer components
- 📞 **WhatsApp Integration** - Direct contact for consultation
- 🎯 **SEO Optimized** - Proper meta tags and semantic HTML
- 🌓 **Theme Support** - Light/dark mode capability

### 📄 Page Structure

```
Home-Solution-Web/
├── index.html              # Homepage with all business divisions
├── pages/
│   ├── kaca-aluminium.html # Glass & Aluminum products
│   ├── baja-ringan.html    # Steel frame & roofing
│   ├── keramik.html        # Ceramics & sanitation
│   ├── furniture.html      # Furniture & home essentials
│   └── tentang-kami.html   # About us page
├── assets/
│   ├── css/
│   │   └── style.css       # Main stylesheet
│   ├── js/
│   │   ├── main.js         # Core JavaScript functionality
│   │   ├── animations.js   # UI animations
│   │   └── page-kaca.js    # Page-specific scripts
├── _header.html            # Reusable header component
├── _footer.html            # Reusable footer component
└── README.md               # This file
```
